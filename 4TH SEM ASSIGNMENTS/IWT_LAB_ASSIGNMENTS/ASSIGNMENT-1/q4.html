Web Technology Basics


Web development refers to the comprehensive process of creating, building, and maintaining websites and web applications that operate online via browsers. It involves a combination of technical tasks, including web design, content development, client-side scripting (using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript), server-side scripting (using languages like Python, PHP, or Node.js), and network security configuration. The field is generally divided into front-end development (the user-facing interface) and back-end development (the server-side logic and database management), with full-stack developers handling both.


In the modern digital landscape, web development is crucial for establishing a functional online presence, enabling e-commerce, and providing interactive experiences. As technology evolves, web developers must ensure that sites are responsive—adapting smoothly to various screen sizes and devices—while also optimizing for speed, accessibility, and search engine optimization (SEO). With the rise of AI tools, the industry continues to evolve, making it a high-demand career field with significant growth projected in the coming years.
